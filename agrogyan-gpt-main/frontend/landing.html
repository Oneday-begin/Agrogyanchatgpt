Before you begin

A couple of good questions.

Do I need an account to ask a question?

You can open the chatbot and ask a question without signing in. Create an account for your farm profile and account-based features.

Where do the answers come from?

From stored agricultural references and selected farming government resolutions. Answers include source links when evidence is available. Coverage and source dates vary.

Will my chats appear on another phone?

The chat sidebar is saved in the browser you use. Signing in on another device does not currently synchronize those conversation threads.
